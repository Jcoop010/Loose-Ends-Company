## How to cancel today
In the workspace: **Billing → Manage Billing** (opens the Stripe customer portal) → cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period; access continues until then. We receive the cancellation event via Stripe webhook and update your workspace entitlement automatically. There is no cancellation fee and no long-term contract.

## Refund policy (proposed — Option A, 14-day good faith)
First-time subscribers may request a **full refund within 14 calendar days** of the first payment, once, no questions asked, via [SUPPORT CONTACT]. Refunds never apply to:
- renewal periods after the first 14 days, or
- requests made after the first refund was granted (one good-faith refund per customer).

After the first 14 days, **no refunds and no proration**; cancellation stops renewal; access continues through the paid period. Chargeback handling: suspected abuse may close the account (see TERMS §6, §8).

This policy was adopted as the proposed draft; the alternative (Option B: strictly no refunds) is retired. **Owner must confirm or amend the 14-day window and the one-refund-per-customer limit before publication.**

## Chargebacks
If a disclosed feature was materially unavailable, we investigate in good faith and respond within 10 business days via [SUPPORT CONTACT] before contesting a chargeback. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in termination.

## Failed payments
Stripe retries; workspace entitlement follows Stripe status (`active` → `past_due` → `unpaid`). On persistent failure, access to premium features is blocked by the entitlement gate (see TERMS §8); data is retained per the Privacy Policy and can be exported during the grace window.

## Scope
U.S. customers. Taxes remitted by the customer where applicable. Currency and card-network fees are the customer's responsibility.
