## How to cancel today
In the workspace: **Billing → Manage Billing** (opens the Stripe customer portal) → cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period; access continues until then. We receive the cancellation event via Stripe webhook and update your workspace entitlement automatically. There is no cancellation fee and no long-term contract.

## Refund options for the owner to choose
**Option A — Good-faith refund window (recommended by preparing agent for review):** first-time subscribers may request a full refund within **14 calendar days** of the first payment, once, no questions asked, via [SUPPORT CONTACT]. After the first 14 days, or for renewal periods, fees are non-refundable. Proration: none; access ends at period end on cancel.

**Option B — No refunds, cancel anytime:** no refunds of any payments, including the first; cancellation stops renewal; access continues through the paid period.

Neither option has been selected. **[OWNER-DECISION REQUIRED — A or B]**

## Chargebacks and failed payments
- Failed payments: Stripe retries; workspace entitlement follows Stripe status (active/past_due/unpaid). Persistent failure eventually removes access (per TERMS §8).
- Chargebacks: if a disclosed feature was materially unavailable, we will investigate in good faith and respond within 10 business days via [SUPPORT CONTACT]. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in termination.

## Not covered by these terms
Taxes (customer's responsibility to remit where applicable); currency conversion; bank or card fees charged by your provider.

## Where this policy applies
U.S. customers. International pricing/VAT collection is out of scope for now.
